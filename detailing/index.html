FAQ

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Our mobile unit carries its own water and power, so we only need a spot to park.

How long does a full detail take?

Usually 3 to 4 hours, depending on vehicle size and condition.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Our coating is rated for about one year with regular maintenance washes.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge. Miami weather changes fast, so we always check the forecast.
