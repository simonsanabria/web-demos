Common questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months. We'll recommend a schedule that fits you.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with most major insurance plans and can check your coverage before your visit.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. We take time to explain every step and offer comfort options so you feel at ease.

Do you see children?

Yes. We welcome patients of all ages, from a child's first visit onward.
